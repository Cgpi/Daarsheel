# Client Data Collection Document

## 1. Project Overview

Please provide the following information about the website.

### 1.1 Company Information

**Required**

- Company name
- Short company name or brand abbreviation
- Tagline or positioning statement
- Business type and primary services
- Official website URL
- Company phone number
- Phone number in clickable telephone format
- Official email address
- Business address
- City, state, and country
- Google Maps link or location coordinates
- Company registration number, if applicable
- Business hours

**Optional**

- Logo in SVG, PNG, or JPG format
- Favicon or browser icon
- Brand colors and approved color codes
- Brand fonts or preferred typography
- Social media profiles
- Company introduction video
- Company brochure or presentation

### 1.2 Website Purpose

- What is the main goal of the website?
- Who is the primary target audience?
- Who are the secondary target audiences?
- What action should visitors take on the site?
- Which services or property types should be emphasized?
- Which markets or cities should be included?
- Are there any competitors or reference websites to follow?

## 12. Client Questionnaire

Please answer the following questions.

### Company

1. What is the official name of the company?
2. What is the short brand name?
3. What is the company tagline?
4. What services does the company provide?
5. What areas or cities does the company serve?

### Website

6. What is the purpose of the website?
7. Who is the target audience?
8. What actions should visitors be able to take?
9. Are there any existing websites or reference designs?
10. What style or mood should the website have?
11. Are there specific colors, fonts, or visual elements to use?

### Content

12. Can the client provide final text for all pages?
13. Should the content be written by the client or by the development team?
14. Are there any text restrictions or legal requirements?
15. Which sections are mandatory?
16. Which pages can be added or removed?

### Projects

17. How many projects should be listed?
18. Which projects are currently available?
19. Which project information should be public?
20. Should projects have separate details pages?
21. How should the price and availability be displayed?
22. Are floor plans and brochures required?
23. Should users be able to enquire about a specific project?

### Contact and Leads

24. What is the primary phone number?
25. What is the email address for enquiries?
26. What should happen after a contact form is submitted?
27. Should leads be sent by email, CRM, WhatsApp, or another platform?
28. Should the contact form include consent and privacy information?
29. Are there any restrictions on storing customer data?

### Assets

30. Does the client have a logo, brand kit, and approved images?
31. Can the images be used for commercial and promotional purposes?
32. Do all images have copyright or licensing permission?
33. Is a company video available?
34. Is a professional photographer or content writer required?

### Timeline

35. What is the preferred launch date?
36. Who provides final approval?
37. How many revision rounds are included?
38. What is the preferred communication and meeting schedule?

## 14. Recommended Next Step

The client should complete the questionnaire and return all required files before development continues. Any missing information should be marked as "Pending" rather than guessed or assumed.

The text, images, prices, contact information, and business claims should be checked and approved by the client before publication.
